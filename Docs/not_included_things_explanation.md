\item{(NOT INCLUDED) Effective number of routes (flow level)}:
    This metric would allow comparison at the route-choice level, showing how route diversity differs between BM and DUE. In particular, it could reveal whether one algorithm tends to concentrate traffic on a smaller number of routes, while the other distributes traffic across a larger set of alternatives.
    Thus, this metric could provide a useful quantitative counterpart to the OD/path-level route-composition plot. However, I decided not to include it in this section because BM and DUE may use different route sets for a given OD pair. For such a metric to provide a meaningful comparison, the two algorithms should ideally be evaluated over the same set of routes. In that case, differences in the metric could be more directly attributed to differences in the algorithms' route-choice behavior. When the route sets differ, however, differences in the metric may result either from differences in the algorithms' route-choice behavior or simply from differences in the available route sets. Consequently, these two effects cannot be disentangled.
